On iPhone

The Thinking Place isn't in the App Store, but it works just as well from your home screen. It takes ten seconds:

  1. Open the web app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The Thinking Place now opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app, and works without a connection.

Good to know

Open it from the home-screen icon rather than from a Safari tab: that way your notes are kept safely, even when Safari clears old website data.

Your notes stay on your iPhone. Export them now and then from Settings to keep a copy.

Daily reminders are only available in the Android app for now.
