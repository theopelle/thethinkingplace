    About

    One thought each day, the same for everyone, drawn in turn from nine traditions: Buddhism, Christianity, Hinduism, Islam, Judaism, Epicureanism, Existentialism, Platonism and Stoicism.

    Read slowly. Sit with the question. Write if you like, in a paper notebook or here; anything you type stays on this device, and you'll find it again in past thoughts.

    Why Many Wells

    For thousands of years, wells were where people gathered and where water was drawn up from the depths. Many traditions saw wisdom the same way: one water, reached through many wells. Each day, Many Wells draws from a different one, from the Upanishads to the Stoics, and leaves you with a question to sit with. The deepest well, as Marcus Aurelius wrote, is within: "if thou wilt ever dig."

    Terms & privacy

    Your notes are yours alone

    Everything you write in Many Wells is stored only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no server copy, so no one, not even the people who run Many Wells, can read, recover or restore your notes.

    This also means that if you clear this browser's data, uninstall the app or lose the device, your notes are gone. Use export on the About page to keep your own copy.

    What we collect

    Nothing. Many Wells uses no analytics, no cookies and no tracking. Your name (if you give one), theme and silence settings are stored on your device alongside your notes. If you report an issue or suggest a reflection, your own email app sends exactly what you choose to write, and we only see that message. Once loaded, the app works entirely offline.

    The texts

    Every passage is quoted from a public-domain translation, credited under "about this passage" with a link to its source. The short context notes and reflection questions are our own. Many Wells presents these traditions side by side for personal reflection; it does not endorse or rank any of them.

    Not advice

    Many Wells is a space for thought, not a substitute for medical, psychological or spiritual care. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
