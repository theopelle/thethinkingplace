    Terms & privacy

    Terms & privacy

    Your notes are yours alone

    Everything you write in Reflect is stored only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no server copy, so no one, not even the people who run Reflect, can read, recover or restore your notes.

    This also means that if you clear this browser's data, uninstall the app or lose the device, your notes are gone. Use export on the About page to keep your own copy.

    What we collect

    Nothing. Reflect uses no analytics, no cookies and no tracking. Your theme and silence settings are stored on your device alongside your notes. Once loaded, the app works entirely offline.

    The texts

    Every passage is quoted from a public-domain translation, credited under "about this passage" with a link to its source. The short context notes and reflection questions are our own. Reflect presents these traditions side by side for personal reflection; it does not endorse or rank any of them.

    Not advice

    Reflect is a space for thought, not a substitute for medical, psychological or spiritual care. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
