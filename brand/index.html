The Thinking Place

Brand guide · October 2026. The logo, colours and type used in the app, the website and the store listings. Files are in this folder.

Logo

Twelve rays, one for each path, around a crescent moon: a thought rising, and a quiet moment at the end of the day. The single teal ray is today's path. The rays never change colour except that one.

Clear spaceKeep at least one ray's length free around the mark.
Smallest size24 px on screen (status bar); below 48 px, use the mark without the tile.
Don'tRecolour the rays, add shadows or outlines, rotate it, or put it on a busy photo.

Type

A thought is set in a book serif. The question is in italics.

Body and interface text in the phone's own sans-serif (San Francisco on iPhone and Mac, Roboto on Android, Segoe UI on Windows), light weight.

Path names in spaced capitals

SerifLiterata, regular and italic (free, SIL Open Font License; files in fonts/) · thoughts, titles, questions
Sans-apple-system, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif · everything else
Why LiterataDesigned for long reading on screens (it is Google Play Books' typeface), with a calm italic. Bundled in the app (about 75 KB), so it looks identical on every phone and works offline.

Voice

CalmShort sentences, no exclamation marks in the interface (the goodbye lines are the exception).
PlainExplain like a good teacher: no jargon without a note, no "unlock", "streak" or "level up".
HonestFree, no ads, no tracking: say it simply, never as a sales pitch.
Names"The Thinking Place" in full; "Thinking" only as the home-screen label. Schools of thought are "paths".
